# Fair tournament scheduler

Course: **Discrete Math for Builders** (Intermediate, 36 hours).

Use graphs and counting to generate and validate a tournament schedule.

Read the modules in [the study map](https://github.com/CS-Geni/curriculum/blob/main/courses/discrete-math.md), then build this on your own machine. The [learning site](https://cs-geni.github.io/public/) is where the lessons live.

## Done when

- [ ] Teams or players are vertices, and games are edges.
- [ ] The schedule gives every participant the matches you promised.
- [ ] A short counting argument shows the schedule is complete.
