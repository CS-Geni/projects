# Design a tiny digital assistant

Course: **Computer Science Without the Jargon** (Beginner, 8 hours).

Create a flowchart and precise instructions for a helpful everyday automation—no code required.

Read the modules in [the study map](https://github.com/CS-Geni/curriculum/blob/main/courses/computer-thinking.md), then build this on your own machine. The [learning site](https://cs-geni.github.io/public/) is where the lessons live.

## Done when

- [ ] A reader who was not in the room can follow the steps without asking a question.
- [ ] The flowchart has a start, an end, and at least one decision.
- [ ] Inputs and outputs are named before the steps.
