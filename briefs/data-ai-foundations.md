# Transparent prediction report

Course: **Data & AI Foundations** (Intermediate, 40 hours).

Build a small model and publish a model card covering data, results, limitations, and appropriate use.

Read the modules in [the study map](https://github.com/CS-Geni/curriculum/blob/main/courses/data-ai-foundations.md), then build this on your own machine. The [learning site](https://cs-geni.github.io/public/) is where the lessons live.

## Done when

- [ ] The question the model answers is one sentence.
- [ ] The write-up says what is in the data and what was left out.
- [ ] Results include one measured outcome and one example the model gets wrong.
- [ ] A model card states the limits and who should not rely on the prediction.
