# Production-ready service

Course: **Shipping & Operating Software** (Intermediate, 26 hours).

Containerize, test, deploy, monitor, and document a small web service.

Read the modules in [the study map](https://github.com/CS-Geni/curriculum/blob/main/courses/shipping-software.md), then build this on your own machine. The [learning site](https://cs-geni.github.io/public/) is where the lessons live.

## Done when

- [ ] The service has a health check.
- [ ] A test runs before the artifact is published.
- [ ] You can tell from a log or metric whether the service is up.
- [ ] A short runbook says how to restart it and what to look at first.

## A note on sources

- [Site Reliability Engineering](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/) (Free to read on official site)
- [The Twelve-Factor App](https://12factor.net/) (MIT)

Use the books for ideas. Do not paste them into your project.
