# Resilient chat service

Course: **Networks from First Principles** (Intermediate, 36 hours).

Build a multi-client chat protocol and test what happens under delay and disconnection.

Read the modules in [the study map](https://github.com/CS-Geni/curriculum/blob/main/courses/computer-networks.md), then build this on your own machine. The [learning site](https://cs-geni.github.io/public/) is where the lessons live.

## Done when

- [ ] Two clients can exchange a line of text through your service.
- [ ] You wrote down the messages in the protocol, including who sends first.
- [ ] You describe what a client does after a delay and after a disconnect.
